## Summary

**Safehouse Patch 3.01** is a small trust-and-community follow-up on top of **3.0 Safehouse**. It adds the **67 Community Ballot** in the login and dashboard footers so players can register a preference on the platform’s ongoing “67” discourse—without leaving the main shell.

## What’s new in 3.01

### 67 Community Ballot (footer)

A discreet footer control lets signed-in players cast one preference per account. The UI is intentionally minimal so it does not compete with Safety or Settings.

| Control | Label | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Strikethrough | ~~6-7~~ | **Vote NO** — you do **not** support “67” on Pixel Place |
| Plain text | 6-7 | **Vote YES** — you **do** support “67” on Pixel Place |

**How to vote**

1. Sign in (ballot is account-bound).
2. Scroll to the footer on **Login** or the **Dashboard**.
3. Tap **~~6-7~~** to vote **NO**, or tap **6-7** (no strikethrough) to vote **YES**.
4. A popup appears with **poll results** (your vote and current YES/NO totals for the community ballot).

Votes are stored on your account in `account_preferences` so they persist across reloads and devices. Reloading the page does **not** clear a vote you have already cast.

> **Note for moderators:** This is a community sentiment poll, not a moderation action. Results may inform future Safehouse policy posts; they do not ban or reward users automatically.

### Auth & safety (carried from 3.0, touched in this patch)

- Email login codes for verified accounts on sign-in.
- “Verify your account” prompt for unverified accounts (routes to Settings).
- Inline sign-in on `/verify` and `/signoutall` without bouncing to the home login card.

### Deploy / platform

- Hosting predeploy runs a **single** `next build` (fixes trace `ENOENT` when `main` and `pay` built in parallel).
- `outputFileTracingRoot` set so Next.js traces from the project lockfile, not the home directory.
