## Summary

First **documented** release in this log. **Safehouse** names the current safety- and trust-focused era of Pixel Place: moderation, reporting, privacy tooling, and calmer account controls sit alongside the core play-create-share loop.

## What Pixel Place is (as of 3.0)

**Pixel Place** is a browser-based game platform by **Mainline Studios**. Players sign in, customize a 3D avatar with skins and accessories, play built-in and community games, build games in **Game Studio**, earn and spend **Pixel Coins**, and connect with friends—all without installing a client.

The live site is served on Firebase Hosting (`pixel-place-823b1`, pay, and status targets). Auth uses Firebase; game and profile data flow through the app’s APIs and Firestore-backed flows.

### Core experience

- **Games** — Catalog of built-in and published titles; mobile beta layout on small screens; keyboard shortcuts (G, C, V, P, F, O, R) on desktop.
- **Game Studio** — Create and publish community games (Studio tab evolving per product direction).
- **Avatar Shop** — Skins, faces, accessories, 3D preview.
- **Pixel Coins** — In-platform economy for shop and features.
- **Friends** — Requests, chat with word blocklists, presence-oriented social hooks.
- **Safety** — Dedicated Safety tab: reporting, appeals context, privacy export, Do Not Disturb presets, and unified account safety settings.
- **Settings** — Sound, accessibility (reduce motion, invert, color-vision filters), mobile vs desktop layout, **visual styles** (Modern, Futuristic, Normal, retro 80s/90s, Low Contrast, **High Contrast**, **Maximalist**, **Minimalist**), secret password themes, language picker.

### Trust & moderation (Safehouse)

- **Pyx** — Content check/filter APIs with Claude backup when Pyx is unavailable; display filtering across user-generated text.
- **Reporting & safety APIs** — User reports, safety endpoints, hardware bans, admin tooling for elevated roles.
- **Privacy** — Safety/privacy panel in Settings; export-oriented privacy flows.
- **Auth hardening** — Firestore-first login path, email verification flow, Google Sign-In, session handling.

### Presentation & brand

- **Mainline beats** — Official Sonauto music beat linked as Mainline Studios (not Pixel Place–only), with a brief AI-generated disclosure in the UI.
- **Brand kit** — Public brand assets; README PDF removed from hosted kit after initial run.
- **Schema / SEO** — Structured data includes Mainline social and music links.
