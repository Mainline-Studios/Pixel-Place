Mission

Premium should not mean paywalled.

Mainline Studios builds software that feels intentional — clear motion, careful craft, and experiences you would expect from a paid product — without putting that quality behind a checkout wall.

We believe free can still mean ambitious. Games you want to stay in. Tools that respect your focus. Interfaces that treat attention as something worth designing for.

That is the through-line across Pixel Place, Pyx AI, Mainline Intelligence, and everything we ship next: providing premium experiences for free.

Craft over clutter

Fewer surfaces, stronger hierarchy, and motion that earns its place.

Open by default

Start in the browser. No install tax just to try the good version.

Made for people

Play, create, and get work done — without feeling like a second-class user.

Approach

How we ship

Small studio, high bar. We iterate in public and keep the best parts free.

  1. 01

    Design the feeling first

    Before features pile up, we lock the mood — brand, motion, and the first impression that says this was made with care.

  2. 02

    Ship real products

    Pixel Place, Pyx AI, and the MI beta are live destinations, not demos. You can play, create, and work in them today.

  3. 03

    Stay with the community

    Feedback on Discord, updates in the open, and music on Treblo — the studio grows with the people who use it.
